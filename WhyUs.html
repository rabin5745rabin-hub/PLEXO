SUPPLY RULES & REGULATIONS

1. Supply Schedule

  • One supply trip will take 3 days.
  • Each trip will cover 24 boats.
  • A total of 10 trips are planned per month.

2. Standard Supply Quantity

Boxes per boat: 2 Boxes

Bottles per box: 12 Bottles

Total per boat: 24 Bottles

Total boats per trip: 24 Boats

Total bottles per trip: 576 Bottles

3. Pricing Details

Shop purchase price: ₹15 per bottle

Customer selling price: ₹20 per bottle

Shopkeeper margin: ₹5 per bottle

4. Monthly Shopkeeper Calculation

Monthly trips: 10 Trips

Total monthly bottles: 5,760 Bottles

Total purchase amount: ₹86,400

Total sales amount: ₹1,15,200

Monthly gross profit: ₹28,800

5. Sales Incentives

  • Sales performance will be reviewed regularly.
  • Shops achieving sales targets may receive extra bottles or special gifts.
  • Incentives will depend on sales performance and company approval.

6. Additional Orders

  • Shops may request additional boxes if required.
  • Extra supply depends on product availability.
  • Additional orders must be confirmed with PLEXO.

7. Delivery & Product Checking

  • Shops should check bottle quantity at delivery.
  • Any shortage or damaged bottles should be reported immediately.
  • Shops should follow the agreed selling price.
